Bitcoin briefly touched $70,000 on Wednesday for the first time since early June, capping a dramatic session that combined Washington’s renewed push for crypto legislation, a surprise intervention in the Treasury market and one of the most violent short squeezes of the summer. President Donald Trump used a White House gathering of crypto executives to demand movement on the stalled CLARITY Act, while revealing that regulators are exploring a compliant pathway for decentralized derivatives exchange Hyperliquid to enter the United States.
