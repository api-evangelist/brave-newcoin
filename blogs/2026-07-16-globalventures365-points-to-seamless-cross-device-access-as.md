GlobalVentures365, a multi-asset online broker founded in 2011, says investor expectations are shifting toward a seamless trading experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Rather than accepting a sophisticated desktop platform alongside a limited mobile app, traders increasingly expect the same tools, functionality, and ease of use wherever they log in. According to the company, this cross-device consistency is becoming a major factor in how investors assess the quality of a trading platform.
