Prediction markets do not have to be glorified coin flips measured in five-minute candles. Some of the most compelling contracts unfold over months or even years, allowing traders to put real conviction behind their views on where crypto is heading. That is the idea behind Zoomex’s Long-Term Prediction Markets, which cover the industry’s biggest unresolved questions, from future asset prices and ETF developments to regulation, adoption, and major market milestones.
